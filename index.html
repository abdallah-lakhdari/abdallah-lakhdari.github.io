About

I am an Early Career Academic Fellow (Research Fellow) in the School of Computer Science and Engineering at UNSW Sydney.

My research explores the design of intelligent software systems that operate reliably and adapt to changing environments. My work has covered service composition, digital trust, and IoT-enabled applications, and now extends to AI-enabled architectures and knowledge-grounded agents.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Sydney. Alongside research, I am actively involved in teaching, student mentoring, and academic–industry collaborations.

Research

My research focuses on intelligent and trustworthy systems, drawing on artificial intelligence, software systems, and cyber-physical environments.

  • Trustworthy intelligent systems: designing AI-enabled systems that remain reliable, transparent and accountable as data, models and operating conditions change.
  • Agentic and knowledge-grounded systems: exploring how AI agents can combine language models and structured knowledge to reason and act across complex information environments.
  • Service and adaptive systems: designing systems that compose, coordinate and adapt services as users, resources and environments change.
  • Cyber-physical and IoT systems: building intelligent systems that connect sensing, software and autonomous devices in real-world environments.

Current directions

My current work builds on a broader research trajectory in adaptive service composition and digital trust, extending these foundations to the design of reliable AI-enabled systems and architectures across a range of settings, combining agents, language models, knowledge graphs, provenance, and deterministic controls.

Teaching & mentoring

My teaching experience spans undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional education across software engineering, cybersecurity, privacy, programming, and related areas of computer science. At UNSW Sydney, I lecture in Software Engineering Fundamentals and contribute to industry-focused AI education. My previous teaching at the University of Sydney includes lecturing, course coordination, curriculum development, and student assessment across a range of computer science subjects.

I have supported the academic and research development of more than 40 students through mentoring undergraduate and postgraduate capstone projects, honors and master's research, and research internships. My experience also includes day-to-day co-supervision of PhD candidates, with contributions spanning research problem formulation, experimental design, technical development, academic writing, and publication preparation.
