About

I am a computer science researcher working across artificial intelligence, intelligent systems, software and services. I am particularly interested in how computational systems can remain reliable, adaptive and trustworthy as they interact with data, people and changing environments.

My research spans trustworthy AI, knowledge-grounded and agentic systems, service computing, digital trust, IoT and cyber-physical systems. It combines algorithmic and systems work with applied research in areas such as smart environments, autonomous services, financial and regulatory intelligence, and human-centred computing.

I am currently a Research Fellow at UNSW Sydney, where I work with the School of Computer Science and Engineering and the FinTech AI Innovation Consortium. I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Sydney.

Research

My work sits across several connected areas of computer science. Rather than treating them as separate silos, I use ideas from AI, software and service systems, data and cyber-physical computing to study dependable intelligent systems.

  • Trustworthy and human-centred AI — reliability, provenance, explainability, privacy, information integrity and human oversight.
  • Agentic and knowledge-grounded systems — LLMs, AI agents, knowledge graphs, ontologies and structured enterprise knowledge.
  • Service and adaptive systems — composition, coordination, software architecture and runtime adaptation under uncertainty.
  • IoT and cyber-physical computing — smart environments, sensing, mobility, drones, ageing-in-place and autonomous services.

Current directions

My current work examines reliable AI-intensive applications in enterprise and regulatory settings, including systems that combine agents, language models, knowledge graphs, provenance and deterministic controls. This builds on earlier work in adaptive service composition, digital trust, information integrity and intelligent cyber-physical services.
